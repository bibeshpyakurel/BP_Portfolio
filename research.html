SLAPBench: Benchmarking Multimodal Large Language Models for Four-Finger SLAP Fingerprint Verification

Bibesh Pyakurel · M. G. Sarwar Murshed

Introduces a benchmark of 7,832 four-finger fingerprint pairs. The study compares multimodal models and shows how prompting affects false accepts, discrimination, and fairness analysis.

Pixel-Level Pavement Distress Assessment Using Instance Segmentation

Logan Dewick · Bibesh Pyakurel · Kong Pheng Yang · Nazim Choudhury · M. G. Sarwar Murshed

Evaluates Mask R-CNN on the field-collected UWGB-StreetCrack dataset. The best model reached 87.04% F1 under the paper’s bounding-box matching protocol, with predicted crack-area fraction close to ground truth.
